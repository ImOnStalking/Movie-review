Yes Man (2008)

Comedy Romance PG-13

Directed by Peyton Reed | Starring Jim Carrey, Zooey Deschanel, Bradley Cooper, John Michael Higgins, Rhys Darby, Danny Masterson, Fionnula Flanagan, Terence Stamp

Runtime: 104 minutes
Release Date: December 19, 2008
Box Office: $223 million worldwide

Our Rating: 7/10

Movie Review

Yes Man is a hilarious comedy starring Jim Carrey as Carl Allen, a man who decides to say "yes" to everything for a year after attending a self-help seminar. This decision leads to a series of outrageous and life-changing adventures.

The film explores themes of spontaneity, personal growth, and the joy of embracing new experiences. Carrey's energetic performance is perfectly complemented by Zooey Deschanel's charm and Bradley Cooper's charisma.

Strengths

  • Jim Carrey's Performance: Carrey delivers one of his best performances in years, balancing humor with genuine emotion.
  • Humorous Situations: The film's premise leads to endlessly funny scenarios that keep the audience laughing.
  • Supporting Cast: Zooey Deschanel and Bradley Cooper provide excellent support.
  • Message: The film promotes positivity and trying new things.

Weaknesses

  • Predictable Plot: The story follows a familiar self-help comedy formula.
  • Pacing: Some scenes feel rushed.
  • Runtime: Could have been tighter.

Final Thoughts

Yes Man is a fun, feel-good comedy that showcases Jim Carrey at his best. It's light-hearted entertainment that leaves you with a smile.

Cast Highlights

  • Jim Carrey as Carl Allen
  • Zooey Deschanel as Allison
  • Bradley Cooper as Peter
