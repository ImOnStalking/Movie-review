2012 (2009)

Sci-Fi Disaster PG-13

Directed by Roland Emmerich | Starring John Cusack, Amanda Peet, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Thandie Newton, Oliver Platt, Tom McCarthy, Woody Harrelson, Danny Glover

Runtime: 158 minutes
Release Date: November 13, 2009
Box Office: $769.7 million worldwide

Our Rating: 6/10

Movie Review

2012 depicts a catastrophic end of the world scenario based on the Mayan calendar. Directed by Roland Emmerich, it follows a writer and his family as they survive global disasters.

The plot involves massive earthquakes, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions triggered by solar flares heating the Earth's core.

Strengths

  • Visual Effects: Spectacular CGI destruction sequences that are visually impressive.
  • Scale: Epic scope of the disasters keeps you engaged.
  • Cast: Solid performances, especially Cusack and Ejiofor.
  • Entertainment Value: Pure popcorn fun with non-stop action.

Weaknesses

  • Plot Logic: Scientific inaccuracies and implausible scenarios.
  • Character Development: Shallow characters and predictable arcs.
  • Pacing: Some scenes drag despite the chaos.

Final Thoughts

2012 is a guilty pleasure disaster movie. It's over-the-top and fun, but don't expect realism.

Overall, this is a 6/10. Entertaining spectacle.

Cast Highlights

  • John Cusack as Jackson Curtis
  • Chiwetel Ejiofor as Adrian Helmsley
  • Amanda Peet as Kate Curtis
  • Woody Harrelson as Charlie Frost
