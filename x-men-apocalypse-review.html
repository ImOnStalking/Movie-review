X-Men Apocalypse (2016)

Sci-Fi Action PG-13

Directed by Bryan Singer | Starring James McAvoy, Michael Fassbender, Jennifer Lawrence, Oscar Isaac, Nicholas Hoult, Rose Byrne, Evan Peters, Sophie Turner, Tye Sheridan, Alexandra Shipp

Runtime: 144 minutes
Release Date: May 27, 2016
Box Office: $543.9 million worldwide

Our Rating: 7/10

Movie Review

X-Men Apocalypse brings back the ancient mutant Apocalypse, awakened in the 1980s, seeking to reshape the world. Directed by Bryan Singer, it features the original X-Men team.

The plot follows Professor X and Magneto as they mentor young mutants against Apocalypse's destructive plans.

Strengths

  • Visual Spectacle: Impressive CGI and action sequences, especially Apocalypse's powers.
  • Ensemble Cast: Strong performances from the returning cast and new additions.
  • Action Sequences: High-energy fights and chases.
  • Character Development: Explores themes of power and legacy.

Weaknesses

  • Plot Pacing: Feels rushed with too many characters.
  • Villain Depth: Apocalypse could be more menacing.
  • Subplots: Some side stories feel underdeveloped.

Final Thoughts

X-Men Apocalypse delivers thrilling action and sets up future stories. It's entertaining but not the best in the series.

Overall, this is a 7/10. Solid superhero fun.

Cast Highlights

  • Oscar Isaac as Apocalypse
  • James McAvoy as Professor X
  • Michael Fassbender as Magneto
  • Jennifer Lawrence as Mystique
